Big-League Agency Pedigree

Before launching Organic Agency, Amantha entered the marketing profession at age 17, cutting his teeth within leading multi-national ad agencies in Sri Lanka. His formative experience includes stints at Lowe LDB and Publicis Groupe, managing high-stakes brand strategy, television scripts, and nationwide campaigns for enterprise accounts.

This dual perspective — institutional Madison Avenue rigor coupled with the agile, hyper-reactive instincts of an active social creator — gives Amantha an unfair advantage in modern brand building.

Amantha Perera founded Organic Agency in Colombo to deliver zero-paid-media growth for consumer brands.

The Organic Marketing Framework

Pillar 1

Cultural Hook Mechanics

Capturing the first 2 seconds through authentic Sri Lankan cultural tropes, unexpected physical staging, and conversational Sinhala or English pacing rather than polished corporate platitudes.

Pillar 2

Commercial Conversion

Bridging high entertainment value directly into real customer action — footfall to physical stores, direct WhatsApp bookings, or retail checkout conversion.

Pillar 3

Zero-Budget Distribution

Engineering content architectures designed for algorithm-driven sharing. When consumers share content in group chats and stories, paid media ad spend becomes completely obsolete.
