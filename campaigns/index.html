CASE STUDY 01

Dinidu Jewellery: "The Staged Proposal"

The Challenge: High-end luxury jewellery marketing in Sri Lanka traditionally relied on static billboard imagery and predictable celebrity endorsement photo shoots, yielding low digital engagement and high customer acquisition costs.

The Creative Concept: Amantha Perera engineered a multi-act social narrative centered around a staged public romantic proposal. The video appeared entirely spontaneous, captivating hundreds of thousands of viewers who engaged intensely with the emotional storyline before the clever brand payoff was revealed.

The Dinidu Jewellery proposal campaign created nationwide social media virality and real showroom ring sales.
Reach

1.8 Million Organic Views

Achieved massive distribution across Instagram Reels and TikTok within 72 hours without a single rupee of paid ad boost.

Engagement

12,000+ Shares & Saves

Triggered thousands of user shares into private WhatsApp groups, peer circles, and widespread user-generated reaction videos.

Commercial Impact

Direct Showroom Ring Sales

Directly converted online hype into high-ticket engagement and wedding ring purchases at Dinidu Jewellery boutiques.

CASE STUDY 02

Driveline Lanka: "The Colombo Street Altercation"

The Challenge: Auto detailing and vehicle maintenance are low-involvement, highly commoditized services where traditional commercials fail to hold consumer attention.

The Creative Concept: Amantha scripted and directed a hyper-realistic, tension-filled roadside confrontation in Colombo. Just as the dispute seemed ready to escalate, the conflict pivoted into an unexpected, comedic dispute over who had the cleaner ceramic-coated vehicle — resolving in an unforgettable brand endorsement for Driveline.

Driveline viral street confrontation campaign — over 2.1 million organic views across social channels.
Virality

2.1 Million Views

Became one of the most shared brand video clips in Sri Lanka's automotive services history.

Brand Recall

Top-of-Mind Detailing Authority

Established Driveline as the most recognized premium detailing provider in the Western Province.

CASE STUDY 03

Upali's by Nawaloka & Sarigama: Cultural Storytelling

Beyond high-octane comedy stunts, Amantha Perera executes rich cultural narratives. Working with Upali's by Nawaloka, campaigns focused on authentic Sri Lankan dining rituals, multi-generational family dining traditions, and local culinary pride. With Sarigama Events, organic community activations brought live music enthusiasts together, filling Colombo concert halls purely through organic digital channels.
